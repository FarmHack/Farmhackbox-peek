One box, four sizes

A Farm Hack Box is not one machine. The same software comes in four sizes, from the phone in a steward’s pocket to a regional anchor that holds the archive, and each size has one job.

3
Regional anchor
Small desktop computer

Regional anchor: models, media, archive, backup receiver. Rasters and WARC are the only genuinely large classes.

  • AMD Ryzen AI Max+ 395 · 64 GB
  • Local AI models, media, archive
  • Receives sealed backups
1 in stewards’ hands
2
Aggregator
Laptop

A T2 is a relationship between farms, so it should only exist where that relationship already does.

  • Its own battery: rides out outages
  • Gathers records from farms that trust it
  • Hosts the fleet’s own network
1 in stewards’ hands
1
Farm or organisation box
Raspberry Pi 5 in a small case

One per organization. Cheap enough to lose, and it is — the replication unit.

  • 8–16 GB memory · 1 TB NVMe
  • 5–10 W: runs on a 50 W solar panel
  • The whole farm stack, offline
13 in stewards’ hands
0
Capture device
A phone, or a Pi Zero

Observation happens where people are. Stewards contribute observations, not server administration.

  • Field records, photos and sound
  • Works offline, syncs to its box
  • The Farm Hack Box app

Where the boxes are

13 Tier 1 boxes are in stewards’ hands, on four continents, alongside a Tier 2 aggregator and a Tier 3 regional anchor. A box counts because a steward holds it; being offline for a while is part of the design, so this map shows where boxes are, not whether they are on.

Why choose self-hosted infrastructure?

Data sovereignty

Your farm data stays on your farm. No cloud lock-in, no subscription fees, no surprises.

Community governed

Built by farmers, for farmers. Steered by the community, funded by grants not VC.

Built for resilience

Works offline, runs on solar, survives internet outages. Infrastructure you can depend on.
