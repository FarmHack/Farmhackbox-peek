Why It Exists

Built around how farm stores actually work

Square and Shopify were designed for stores that sell their own inventory to anonymous customers. A farm store is different — and the software should be too.

You Own the System

Runs on a Raspberry Pi on your premises. Your data in your database. No company can raise prices, change terms, or shut down your Saturday sales.

Works Offline

Rural internet is unreliable. Sales, inventory, and customer lookups work without a connection. Cloud sync happens in the background when you're back online.

Built for Consignment

Multi-vendor consignment with individual commission rates, vendor color coding, and automated settlement reports — not an afterthought, but the core model.

Designed to Be Modified

A single HTML file and a few Python scripts. Any developer can read the whole thing in an afternoon. Any farm can fork it, change it, share improvements back.

Feature Overview

Everything a farm store actually needs

Every item here was built because a real farm store needed it. Features that would require enterprise add-ons elsewhere are first-class here.

Multi-Vendor Consignment

Each vendor has a profile, commission rate, and color badge. Products carry vendor attribution through the whole system — grid, cart, receipt, settlement. One click to see what each vendor is owed.

Core Feature

Weight-Based Pricing

Tap a weight-based product and a live scale panel opens — real-time weight from a connected scale, tare control, and automatic line total calculation.

Hardware Integrated

NFC Customer Cards

Tap a card, customer is identified, discount applied, payment pre-filled. The same card can unlock the store door outside of staffed hours. One card, two functions.

Hardware Integrated

Attended + Kiosk Mode

Full cashier interface for staff. Simplified self-service kiosk with larger touch targets, floating cart, English/Spanish toggle, and staff-only options hidden automatically.

Dual Mode

Barcode Label Printing

ZPL labels on a Zebra ZD411 via a direct USB bridge. Single product or batch-print the whole inventory. Ten seconds from click to label. Fully configurable dimensions and layout.

Hardware Integrated

Receipt Printing

ESC/POS thermal receipt printing. Receipts group line items by vendor, show member discounts, payment method, and customer name. Configurable width and footer text.

Hardware Integrated

Mobile Inventory App

Walk the barn with your phone. Take a photo, enter stock count, save offline. Back at WiFi, sync everything to the POS — photos, counts, and all. Camera barcode scanning included.

Companion App

Vendor Settlement

Per-vendor settlement report: gross sales, commission, net payable. Mark Paid records the settlement. What takes a spreadsheet and an hour takes five minutes here.

Core Feature

SNAP/EBT + Matching

Tracks EBT payments alongside card and cash. SNAP double-dollar matching programs work via the prepaid balance — spend $10 EBT, receive $10 store credit in one step.

Supported

Commons Funding Pools

Optional checkout contribution prompts for community-administered funds. Climate resilience, farm worker support, open infrastructure — one tap to contribute, no dark patterns.

In Development

farmOS Integration

Admin panel for farmOS connection is built and ready. Designed to connect sales to harvest records — what was sold comes out of what was harvested. A continuous field-to-sale record.

In Development

Local + Cloud Data

Local PostgreSQL on the Pi for resilience. Optional cloud mirror for backup. Shareable URL to add a second terminal or market-stand device with no manual configuration.

Farm Hack Box Ecosystem

One box, many apps — all connected

The POS is the commerce layer of the Farm Hack Box. It shares the same data infrastructure as every other app on the box — so a harvest in farmOS can become a POS product, and a CSA membership can become a customer account.

farmOS Integration

Field records, harvest logs, and input tracking in farmOS connect to POS sales and inventory. A harvest entry becomes a product. A sale deducts from inventory. One continuous record from field to sale.

Customer Relationships

Five account types for real community

Commercial systems think of customers as transaction records. Farm stores have members, share holders, neighbors, and regulars. The customer model reflects that.

Regular

Basic profile with contact info and purchase history. Useful for any customer you want to track over time.

Member

Carries a discount percentage — 10% for a CSA subscriber, for example. Applied automatically on NFC card tap.

CSA Account

Community-supported agriculture arrangement. Load credit at the start of the season, draw it down each visit.

Store Card

Prepaid balance like a gift card — or loaded by a neighbor as a gift. Powers SNAP matching programs.

House Account

Charge purchases for trusted regulars, settle weekly or monthly. Hidden in kiosk mode — staff-verified only.

Open Infrastructure

Not a product you subscribe to

This is infrastructure farms build and own together. The Farm Hack POS is a foundation — designed to be understood, modified, and improved by the farms that use it.

  • Single HTML file — readable by any developer in an afternoon
  • Python bridges for hardware — no proprietary drivers
  • PostgreSQL for data — exportable anytime, no lock-in
  • Part of the Farm Hack Box — shares data with every other app on the box
  • Every improvement shared back to the community
  • Fork it, adapt it, give it a different name if it fits better
